Our Story

Changing how North America
brews at home.

Walk into any Canadian or American kitchen and you'll find a drip coffee maker. It's been the default for decades — simple, convenient, and familiar. But here's the problem: drip coffee has never been the full story of what coffee can be.

Mandike Coffee was founded in Ottawa with a simple belief: every home deserves the richness, the crema, the ritual of a real espresso. North America has been sleeping on espresso at home — and we're here to wake it up.

The numbers tell the story. Drip machines still make up over half of all home coffee appliances in North America. Only about 11% of daily coffee drinkers use an espresso machine at home. Yet specialty coffee consumption has hit a 14-year high — people are falling in love with better coffee when they go out. They just haven't been able to bring it home.

We're changing that. Our machines are designed for Canadian kitchens — easy enough for weekday mornings, precise enough for weekend experimentation. From our roastery-neighbourhood workshop in Ottawa, we build machines that make espresso approachable, not intimidating.

— The Mandike Team, Ottawa, Ontario
